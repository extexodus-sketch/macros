maCros Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Version 2026-10-15

This policy explains what personal information maCros collects, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). The person responsible for your data is the maCros operator, reachable at extexodus@gmail.com.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Your email address, and your name and profile photo if you sign in with GoogleTo create your account and let you sign in
Food logs (foods, amounts, meal, time), your days, your daily targets, your own foods and recipes, and app settingsTo show your totals and keep them in sync across your devices. This is the app's whole purpose.
The date you accepted these terms and confirmed you're 18+To record your consent

Food logs and targets can say something about your health, so we treat them as sensitive personal information and process them only with your consent, which you give when you create an account.

We don't collect: your location, contacts, or payment details. We don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

Voice input

If you tap the microphone, your browser's built-in speech service turns speech into text. Depending on your browser, that is Google (Chrome) or Apple (Safari), under their own privacy policies. maCros receives only the resulting text, never the audio.

Where your data is stored

Your account and data are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), and a copy is kept on your device so the app works offline. Firebase servers are located outside the Philippines (the database is hosted in Singapore). Google acts as our service provider and protects data under its own security and privacy commitments.

Who can see it

  • Only you can read or change your data through the app. Security rules block every other user.
  • As the administrator of the Firebase project, the maCros operator has technical access to the database. We won't view your data except to fix a problem you report, with your permission, or when the law requires it.
  • We never sell, rent or share your personal information for marketing.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account) permanently erases your account and all your data from our database. Copies on your devices are removed when you delete or sign out and clear site data.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you can ask to be informed about, access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, and to object to or withdraw consent for its processing. Most of these you can do yourself in the app: edit or delete entries, download a backup (a portable copy), or delete your account. For anything else, email us. If you believe your rights have been violated, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access is locked to your signed-in account. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong password and keep your device locked. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we'll notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

Age

maCros is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a minor has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the version and date above, and the app will ask you to review and accept it.
